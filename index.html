Introduction

Who I am and how the internship went.

My name is Muhammad Shoaib, and I am a third-year BS English Language Teaching (ELT) student at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro. As part of my academic program, I completed an 80-hour teaching internship at Ideal Public High School. This internship gave me the opportunity to gain practical experience and understand the working environment of a school.

During the initial days, I observed experienced teachers and learned about their instructional approaches, lesson delivery, and classroom activities. These observations helped me understand how teachers present lessons and deal with students of different learning abilities. Gradually, I became involved in classroom activities and started performing my assigned responsibilities.

Throughout the internship, I participated in teaching activities, helped students understand their lessons, and assisted teachers in maintaining a positive learning environment. I also observed how teachers used textbooks, examples, questions, and discussions to make lessons more effective.

This experience improved my understanding of teaching practices, my communication and presentation skills, and my confidence. It helped me connect my academic knowledge with real classroom experience and understand my future profession as an English language teacher.

Internship objectives

I set out to develop professional teaching abilities and gain firsthand experience of the educational environment. My main objectives were:

  • To understand the structure and daily functioning of a school environment.
  • To develop the ability to prepare and organize effective lesson plans.
  • To practice teaching English grammar, vocabulary, reading, and writing skills.
  • To learn how to explain difficult concepts through simple language and relevant examples.
  • To understand the different learning abilities and educational needs of students.
  • To explore teaching aids and classroom resources for improving the learning process.
  • To develop skills in asking questions, evaluating student understanding, and providing constructive feedback.
  • To build professional ethics, responsibility, patience, and confidence as a future English language teacher.
